Arduino Workshop

24, 25 and 26 Shrawan 2083 (as printed on the workshop banner) · Kantipur Engineering College, Dhapakhel, Lalitpur

A three-day hands-on Arduino workshop. Participants wired circuits on breadboards connected to Arduino boards and worked through the exercises on their laptops, with a facilitator guiding the room.

New to Arduino? Start with the official Arduino documentation and other learning resources.

Maze Solver Workshop

1 and 2 Falgun 2082, from 11:00 AM (as printed on the banner) · Kantipur Engineering College, Dhapakhel, Lalitpur

A two-day workshop on maze-solving robots. The banner credits Broadway Infosys as title sponsor and the session ran under the KEC LITE name. The club also ran a Maze Solver Competition under the same banner.

3D Workshop

A hands-on session in 3D design. Participants followed a live demonstration on the classroom display and worked along on their own laptops.
