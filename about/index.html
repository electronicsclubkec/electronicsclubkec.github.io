About KEC Electronics Club

A student electronics club associated with Kantipur Engineering College, Dhapakhel, Lalitpur, Nepal.

What we do

The club brings students together to design, build and test electronic systems, and to learn the fundamentals behind them. Activities include projects, workshops and events.
