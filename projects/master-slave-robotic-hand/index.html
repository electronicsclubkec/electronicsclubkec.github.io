Master–Slave Robotic Hand Using Flex Sensors

A KEC Electronics Club student project.

Problem

Traditional robotic hands often require complex control systems or dedicated input devices. This project explores a more intuitive approach in which the movements of a human hand are replicated by a robotic hand using a wearable glove.

Components and technologies

  • Flex sensors to detect finger bending
  • Arduino Uno or Arduino Nano as the controller
  • Robotic hand structure with articulated fingers
  • Servo motors to control finger movements
  • Resistors for flex sensor voltage-divider circuits
  • Jumper wires, breadboard, and power supply
  • Wearable glove for mounting the sensors

Engineering challenges

  • Calibrating flex sensors to accurately measure different finger positions.
  • Mapping sensor readings to appropriate servo angles.
  • Coordinating multiple servo motors for smooth, synchronized movement.
  • Managing power consumption and ensuring stable servo operation.
  • Designing a lightweight, comfortable glove with secure sensor placement.

Result

A master–slave robotic hand prototype in which flex sensors mounted on a wearable glove detect finger movements and send corresponding signals to a controller. The controller drives servo motors to reproduce those movements on the robotic hand, demonstrating a practical application of sensor-based human–robot interaction.
