KEC Electronics Club · Kantipur Engineering College, Dhapakhel, Nepal

Ideas are easy. Making them work is the fun part.

We are a student electronics community at Kantipur Engineering College in Lalitpur. We build circuits, test them, break them, and learn why they behaved that way.

What the club is

KEC Electronics Club is a place for students to learn electronics by building things: sensors, microcontroller projects, robots and tools. Members share what they learn through projects, workshops and events.
